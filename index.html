Understanding Inclusive Technology

Inclusive technology helps people with different abilities and backgrounds access digital tools, information, education, and workplace opportunities. Explore six important topics that help build a more accessible digital world.

Topic 01

Accessibility Features

Accessibility features are settings and functions in computers, mobile devices, applications, and websites that help people with different abilities use technology. They make digital content easier to see, hear, understand, and navigate.

Why It Is Important

These features reduce barriers and allow users to complete tasks more independently. They can also make technology more comfortable and convenient for everyone.

Examples

  • Text enlargement and screen magnification
  • High-contrast display and color adjustments
  • Voice control and speech recognition
  • Keyboard navigation and captions

Benefits

  • Improved usability
  • Greater independence
  • Easier access to digital information
Key Idea: Technology should be adaptable to the different needs of its users.
Topic 02

Assistive Technologies

Assistive technologies are devices, software, and equipment that help people with disabilities perform activities, communicate, learn, and work. These tools provide additional ways to interact with computers and other digital devices.

Why It Is Important

Assistive technologies support independence and participation in school, work, and everyday activities. They help users overcome barriers that may prevent them from using standard technology.

Examples

  • Screen readers that read text aloud
  • Speech-to-text software for dictation
  • Screen magnifiers for enlarged content
  • Alternative keyboards and adaptive switches
  • Hearing assistance and communication tools

Benefits

  • Supports communication
  • Encourages participation
  • Improves access to education and employment
Key Idea: Assistive tools help people use technology in ways that meet their needs.
Topic 03

Digital Inclusion

Digital inclusion means ensuring that everyone has meaningful opportunities to access and use digital technology. It includes access to devices and the internet, digital skills, accessible services, and support for people who face barriers to participating in the digital world.

Why It Is Important

Many essential services, learning resources, and job opportunities are now available online. Digital inclusion helps people participate in society without being excluded because of cost, location, disability, or limited experience.

Examples

  • Digital literacy training
  • Affordable internet and device access
  • Accessible online learning
  • Technology support for underserved communities
  • Inclusive digital government services

Benefits

  • More learning and job opportunities
  • Greater participation in society
  • Stronger and more connected communities
Key Idea: Everyone deserves an opportunity to participate in the digital world.
Topic 04

Accessible Website

An accessible website is designed so people with different abilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with its content. It considers readable text, meaningful image descriptions, keyboard navigation, clear headings, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

Why It Is Important

An accessible website allows more people to find information and use online services. It also improves the experience for mobile users, older adults, and people using different devices or internet connections.

Examples

  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Clear headings and descriptive links
  • Readable text and sufficient color contrast
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Captions and transcripts for media

Benefits

  • Better usability
  • Access for a wider audience
  • More inclusive online services
Key Idea: A website should be usable by people with different abilities and technologies.
Topic 05

Equal Access

Equal access means giving people fair opportunities to use technology, digital information, online services, and workplace resources. It involves identifying and removing barriers that may prevent individuals from participating fully.

Why It Is Important

Equal access helps create fairer learning and working environments. People should be able to access important information and opportunities without unnecessary technology-related barriers.

Examples

  • Accessible job applications
  • Online training materials for all employees
  • Software compatible with assistive tools
  • Reasonable workplace accommodations
  • Accessible public information and services

Benefits

  • Fairer participation
  • More employment and learning opportunities
  • A more supportive workplace culture
Key Idea: Everyone deserves a fair chance to access digital resources and participate.
Topic 06

Inclusive Digital Practices

Inclusive digital practices are methods used to create, share, and manage digital content while considering the diverse needs of users. They include accessible documents, clear communication, inclusive collaboration, and regular accessibility checks.

Why It Is Important

Planning for accessibility from the beginning helps prevent exclusion and makes digital information easier to use. Inclusive practices also encourage respect, participation, and collaboration among people with different needs.

Examples

  • Add captions to videos and recorded meetings
  • Write clear and understandable content
  • Provide descriptions for meaningful images
  • Use headings and lists in documents
  • Check keyboard access and color contrast

Benefits

  • Better understanding of information
  • Stronger collaboration
  • More inclusive digital environments
Key Idea: Consider different users from the beginning when creating digital content.
